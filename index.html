Crane orientation

How to use the crane

Move the crane with WASD, lift with Space, or open any container directly to read its contents. Camera controls are separate.

W A S D

Move the gantry and trolley. Movement has weight — lead your stops.

↑ ← ↓ →R

Pan the view with these keys or the visible arrow buttons. Drag with the mouse to rotate; R recenters. Camera controls never move the crane.

SPACE

Lower and lock, lift, then lower and release over the dock. Snap assist is forgiving.

CLICKTAP

Open any labelled container to read its contents without moving the crane.

ESC

Close cargo panels. Tap the Action button on touch screens.
